# RAWCLICHumanoidRobots

What a humanoid robot is made of, by category of humanoid robot, through 2040. The research of 2026 defines six
categories, from compact research robots to defense and public safety. Each category has one anchor robot, and
that robot's parts list stands for the category. Every mass in a parts list has a minimum, a mode and a maximum.

**New here? Start with [`documentation/claude/METHODOLOGY.md`](documentation/claude/METHODOLOGY.md)**, then open
the numbered scripts at the top of the project in order. Where the work stands is in
[`documentation/claude/HANDOVER.md`](documentation/claude/HANDOVER.md).

## Running it

Open the folder `RAWCLICHumanoidRobots` itself in Positron (File → Open Folder), not the GitHub folder above it,
so that the project's `.venv` is used. Then press Run on each stage, in order. None takes an argument; every
setting is in `src/params_schema.py`.

| Stage | Full path | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 00 | `…/GitHub/RAWCLICHumanoidRobots/00_parameters.py` | Checks the settings, says which input files are on disk, writes `params.xlsx` and `documentation/claude/PARAMETER_REFERENCE.md`. |
| 01 | `…/GitHub/RAWCLICHumanoidRobots/01_schemas.py` | Reads and checks every input (the workbook, the shared lists, the element recipes, the register, each robot type's parts file, the sibling projects' files) and draws into `figures/schemas/`, every page 16 × 9 inches: the overview (how the model works; the six robot types), a notes page, the faults and checks, and for each robot type taken apart its robots and where its kinds of part sit (types 1 to 4 since 2026-10-30; types 3 and 4 over their robots of every parts file). Pages only of the robot types `output.robot_types_with_pages` names (types 1 to 6 since 2026-11-01, EVIDENCE.md E16.6). |
| 02 | `…/GitHub/RAWCLICHumanoidRobots/02_composition.py` | Draws what each robot type is made of, `composition.draws` times (200,000; Monte Carlo): its materials (one fixed list, each name what it is: copper, brass, nickel, the aluminium, magnesium and titanium alloys, steel, stainless steel, electrical steel, NdFeB magnets, battery cells, boards and components, plastics, fibre composites, ceramics, other; EVIDENCE.md E19), its elements of interest (only those recovered as elements: copper and nickel each its metal whole, the aluminium, magnesium and titanium alloys the metal they hold (their whole mass in the materials, EVIDENCE.md E24); the magnets, the battery cells and the boards and components their metals of interest; steel, brass, plastics and the rest stay materials), its part groups and body regions. A robot type some of whose robots are taken apart is drawn part by part over them (each draw one of them, a robot in two versions each as often, each from its own rows in the parts file it stands in, with that file's shared numbers): type 1 over its 30 robots with a mass (the PrimeBOT Q1 has none since 2026-11-01, EVIDENCE.md E12.4), type 2 over 96 of its 97 (its batches 1, 2 and 2b and its robots on wheels; the Xialan S0 has no mass); type 3 over all 164 of its robots with a mass (the Reflex has none since 2026-11-01: its catalogue's "0.0" is not stated; the hydraulic Neo1 since 2026-11-04, EVIDENCE.md E27.7), type 4 over all 124 of its robots with a mass (their robots taken apart in types 1 and 2, and their batches 1 to 3: E11.7, E12.6, E15.8), type 5 over all 11 and type 6 over all 5 (since 2026-11-04 with the hydraulic Atlas (old) and Nadia, their actuators as Moog's ISA in two versions, their power unit, oil and hoses a kind of their own: E27.7). Each type's notes page says how many of its robots rest on templates, their joints or battery estimated from their mass (E13); since E14 (2026-11-01) 21 robots and versions take their joints (four more their arms) from their makers' own model files or pages. A type's draws come from its parts once every robot of it with a mass is taken apart (types 1, 2, since batch 3 type 4, and since 2026-11-04 types 3, 5 and 6), from the workbook until then. Every statistic it writes or draws is over the draws that hold the quantity, with their share ("held in"): a 0 is not part of a distribution, and the draws keep their zeros for sums (METHODOLOGY.md, "A 0 is not part of a distribution"). Writes each robot type's draws and summary into `data/consolidated/<n>_<name>/` and each type taken apart part by part into `data/parts_taken_apart/<n>_<name>/`. Draws, for the robot types `output.robot_types_with_pages` names (types 1 to 4): into `figures/composition/` a type taken apart its notes pages, its elements six a page (each split by the material it comes from, no text over a panel's bars: EVIDENCE.md E26), each element's mean by material in a table of its own, its materials nine a page (each material, then its grades, alloys or magnet classes; since 2026-11-02, "the material is much too detailed", "Yes, build it": the steels and the aluminium alloys as their material only, their grades on the pages of where each sits and in the alloys table) and its alloys and grades in a table (what each holds by its recipe, its kg in one robot); into `figures/parts_taken_apart/<n>_<name>/` its two figures of where its critical elements sit, their notes once on pages of their own, and, in `sources/`, where copper, the magnets, each steel and each aluminium alloy sit. Stops on any fault of the inputs, before writing anything, and on any draw that does not add up or is below zero; below zero where a remainder of the robot drawn is (a frame, a weighed pack's housing), it reports instead, on the type's notes page (EVIDENCE.md E21). While it runs it says each step as it begins and its time when it ends (reading, checking, each robot type drawn, every tenth robot of a type taken apart, the check that the draws add up, writing and pages), and last "02 done in …" or "02 stopped after …: nothing is written". |
| 03 | `…/GitHub/RAWCLICHumanoidRobots/03_comparison.py` | Reads the draws of 02 and draws the robot types side by side into `figures/comparison/`: by material, element of interest, part group and body region. After robot type 6 (Matthias, 2026-10-29). |

`…` is `/Users/rm/Library/Mobile Documents/com~apple~CloudDocs/Documents`. Several projects have numbered files
with the same numbers: check that the file in front of you is in `RAWCLICHumanoidRobots` before pressing Run.

The tests run from a terminal in the project folder:

```bash
./.venv/bin/python -m unittest discover -s tests
```

## Setup

Python 3.14.4 from pyenv, in a virtual environment in the project folder. No conda.

```bash
~/.pyenv/versions/3.14.4/bin/python3 -m venv .venv
```

```bash
./.venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
```

`.vscode/settings.json` makes `.venv` Positron's interpreter and activates it in every terminal. The versions in
`requirements.txt` are pinned; do not change them without asking.

## What is on GitHub

https://github.com/MattRoess/RAWCLICHumanoidRobots, public, branch `main`. It holds the code and the documents
only. Not in the repository, by `.gitignore`: the workbooks and every other `.xlsx`, everything the stages write
(the figures, the draws in `data/consolidated/` and `data/parts_taken_apart/`, `params.xlsx`, `documentation/claude/PARAMETER_REFERENCE.md`), and
the earlier model `HumanoidRobotsMC/`. A fresh clone cannot run 01 to 03 until `data/raw/humanoid_robots_2040_workbook.xlsx`,
`data/researched/shared_lists.xlsx` and `data/researched/element_recipes.xlsx` are supplied separately, and
each `data/researched/<n>_<name>/parts_taken_apart.xlsx` is written by pressing Run on its type's builder, `tools/<n>_<name>/make_parts_taken_apart.py`.
